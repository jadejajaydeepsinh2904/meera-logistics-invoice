V58 ACCOUNTING + DATA ISOLATION FINAL AUDIT

MAIN FIXES

1. Party payment allocation is now deterministic
- Party payment can be linked directly to Invoice.
- Trip-linked receipt maps to that Trip's Invoice.
- Old/unlinked receipts are allocated FIFO only once.
- One receipt can no longer be counted against every later invoice.
- Invoice received/pending values in Party Khata use backend allocation.
- Unallocated excess is kept as Party Advance/Credit.

2. Receive Payment form
- Party -> Invoice dropdown.
- Only that Party's invoices are shown.
- Invoice pending amount is visible.
- Selecting invoice prefills current pending amount.
- Blank Invoice means Party Advance / Auto FIFO.
- Invoice/Party and Trip/Party mismatches are rejected by backend.

3. Supplier accounting consistency
- Dashboard/Reports Supplier Payable now matches Supplier Khata calculation:
  Truck Entries + PM Bill supplier payable.
- Supplier paid/pending remain company scoped.

4. Multi-company hardening
- Critical runtime updates/deletes are scoped with company_id.
- Accounting audit checks cross-company parent/child links.
- Party/Trip/Invoice payment links are validated inside the logged-in company.

5. Reports & Audit
- New "Run Full Accounting Audit" button.
- Checks invoice/payment links, orphan rows, missing Truck/Supplier master,
  supplier/truck mismatch, cross-company links, and accounting totals.
- Shows Billing, Party Received/Outstanding, Supplier Payable/Paid/Pending,
  unallocated credit, error/warning counts.

6. Data safety
- Existing payments are not deleted or rewritten.
- New party_payments.invoice_id is added lazily once.
- Old payments continue to work through FIFO compatibility allocation.
- D1 schema_version remains 53; V58 adds only a small lazy accounting column/index.

- V55 clean Supplier Khata.
