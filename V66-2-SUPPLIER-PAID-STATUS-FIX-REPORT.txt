V66.2 SUPPLIER PAID STATUS FIX

Issue:
Recent Trip PAID/PENDING stamp was incorrectly checking Party Invoice payment status.
So even after paying the Supplier, the Trip card could remain PENDING.

Fix:
- Trip card PAID/PENDING now checks Supplier payable vs Supplier payments.
- Exact Trip-linked supplier payments update the Trip immediately.
- Older/unlinked Supplier payments are allocated FIFO to matching Supplier/Truck Trips.
- If Supplier payable is fully paid, card shows PAID.
- If any Supplier balance remains, card shows PENDING.
- Trip Details > Supplier tab uses the same allocated Supplier payment amount.
- V66 desktop design retained.
- V65 LR fix retained.
- Backend/Worker schema unchanged.
